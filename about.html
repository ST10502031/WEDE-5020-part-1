About Abby Luxe Hair

Quality and affordable wigs and hair care products for women who love timeless beauty.

Our Story

Abby Luxe Hair is a small business which sells wigs and hair care products. The business was set up to offer customers quality hair products at affordable prices, with a focus on customers who have an interest in hair, beauty and maintaining good looks without paying luxury prices.

As the business has grown, having a single professional home online has become essential — a place where every customer can find our full range, learn who we are, and reach out directly.

Our Mission

To offer customers quality and affordable wigs and hair care products, helping them look good and feel confident and comfortable with their looks.

Our Vision

To become a recognised brand in the field of hair and beauty — known for quality products, excellent service, and prices that stay affordable as we grow.

The Unboxing Experience

Every order arrives beautifully packaged — because feeling confident starts the moment your parcel lands on your doorstep. From our signature pink box to the satin dust bag and handwritten thank-you card, we want every customer to feel the luxury before they even try the hair on.

Who We Serve

Abby Luxe Hair is proud to serve a community built around beauty, confidence and self-expression.

  • Women who wear wigs regularly
  • Youth with an interest in beauty and hair
  • Professional women seeking convenient, quality hair products
  • Consumers looking for affordable, high-quality wigs
  • Anyone who discovered us through social media and wants to know more
